Slope field

Use x and y with + − * / ^, and sin, cos, tan, exp, ln, sqrt, abs, pi, e. Products like 2xy work.

Segment spacing

Drag the point, or tap the plot to place it. Arrow keys also work when the plot is focused. While this is on, scroll the page from outside the plot.

Explicit or implicit, such as y = x^2 + C or x^2 + y^2 = C. Use the slider to change C. Without an “=”, the expression is set equal to 0. Separate several equations with “;”.
