Approximating the area under a curve with rectangles

This approximation gets better as \(N\) gets bigger, so we conclude that the area must equal to

\[ \lim_{N \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^N f(x_i)\Delta x = \int_0^1 f(x) \dif{x}. \]

This last integral is called a definite integral.

Rectangles use right endpoints: \(x_i = i/N\), so the \(i\)-th rectangle has height \(f(x_i) = (i/N)^2\).
